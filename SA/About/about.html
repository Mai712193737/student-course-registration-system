About us

Who we are

We are a university portal that makes course registration simple for students and easy to manage for administrators.

What we do

  • Show available courses and their details
  • Let students register or drop courses with a capacity check
  • Display each student’s weekly schedule in one place
  • Give administrators the tools to manage courses and seats

Our vision

A registration process that is clear, fair, and stress-free, that saves time and effort, so students can spend their time on learning instead of paperwork.
